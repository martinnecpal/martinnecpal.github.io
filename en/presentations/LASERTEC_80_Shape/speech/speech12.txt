SLIDE 12 — LASERTEC 80 vs. 210 Shape: Size Comparison
======================================================

The LASERTEC 80 Shape belongs to a product family. Its larger counterpart is the LASERTEC 210 Shape. This table shows the key differences.

In terms of working volume, the 210 Shape is dramatically larger: 1800 mm in X versus 800 mm, 2100 mm in Y versus 500 mm. The five-axis table diameter grows from 200 or 400 mm on the 80 Shape to 1850 mm on the 210 Shape. Maximum table load increases from 40 kg to 10 tonnes.

However, the LASERTEC 80 Shape has a clear advantage in dynamics. Its rapid traverse in X and Y is 120 metres per minute, compared to only 60 and 40 metres per minute for the 210 Shape. The 80 Shape achieves over 1.2 g acceleration, which is not specified for the larger machine. In other words, the 80 Shape is significantly faster and more agile for small to medium workpieces.

Both machines use the same fiber laser technology with the same power options — 100 or 200 watts.

The weight difference is enormous: 7 tonnes for the 80 Shape versus 42 tonnes for the 210 Shape. The installation footprint grows from 4.5 by 6 metres to 10 by 12 metres.

In summary: the LASERTEC 80 Shape is the right choice for precision parts, cutting inserts, and injection molds of medium size — which is exactly the application domain at CE5AM MTF STU Trnava. The 210 Shape is designed for large automotive stamping dies and body panels.
