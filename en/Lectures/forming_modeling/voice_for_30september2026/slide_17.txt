Video: Ansys Forming

Let us see it in motion. This is an introductory video from Ansys. While watching, follow three things: the blank, the blank holder with draw beads and, at the end, the colour display of the forming limit diagram.
Did you see the colours on the part? Green is safe, yellow is marginal, red means a crack. That is the forming limit diagram projected onto the geometry – the most common result of a sheet metal stamping simulation.
