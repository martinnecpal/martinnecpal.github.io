Working with DEFORM: Pre → Simulation → Post

This is how you will work with DEFORM in the lab – three programs.
The preprocessor, where you define the objects, their geometry and mesh, the material, tool motion, inter-object conditions – friction and heat transfer – stopping criteria and the number of steps. From this you generate the database: the KEY file becomes the DB file. The database is the entire simulation – back it up.
Then the simulation engine performs the FEM calculation with automatic remeshing. It writes message and log files; learn to read them, because that is where you will see convergence problems and remeshing.
And the postprocessor, where you look at state variables, the force–stroke curve, point tracking, flow lines and damage.
On the right are the object types. Rigid – tools that do not deform, optionally with heat transfer. Plastic – the workpiece, rigid-viscoplastic. Elastic – for tool stress analysis. Elasto-plastic – for springback and residual stresses. Porous – for powders and sintered materials.
And DEFORM knows real machine types: hydraulic, mechanical and screw presses and hammers, where the tool motion is governed by energy rather than by a prescribed speed.
