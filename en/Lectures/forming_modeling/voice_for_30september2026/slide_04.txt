Where today's lecture fits

A quick recap of last week's modelling chain. We start with a real process, idealise it into a physical model, describe it with equations – that is the mathematical model – and then solve it, either analytically or numerically with software. At the end comes verification and validation by experiment.
Today we deal with steps three and four – the highlighted boxes. Part I of the lecture is the analytical solution of the mathematical model. Parts II to IV are numerical models and software.
On the right you can see how this connects to the rest of the course: next week material models, then preprocessing and examples of sheet and bulk forming.
And in the lab sessions: DEFORM. So today's Part III is your toolkit for the whole semester.
