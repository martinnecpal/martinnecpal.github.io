The Regular and Mixture switch decides how many sets of data you need. For forming and machining one phase is enough: the material is a Regular material with one flow curve, one conductivity and so on. For heat treatment the material changes during the simulation – austenite becomes ferrite, pearlite, bainite or martensite. Then you define a Mixture: each phase has its own properties and DEFORM averages them with the volume fractions, as in the simple mixture rule. On top of that the Transformation button holds the inter-phase data: the kinetics, the latent heat released, the volume change, and transformation plasticity. The picture shows why this matters: a carburised and quenched gear with the martensite fraction. Without a mixture, you cannot get that picture, nor the distortion and residual stress that come from it.
