Rolled sheets are not isotropic: rolling creates a texture, and the sheet behaves differently along the rolling direction, across it and through the thickness. Hill proposed in nineteen forty-eight the simplest extension of von Mises for such orthotropic materials – a quadratic function with six constants, F, G, H for the normal stress differences and L, M, N for shear. If you set F, G and H equal and L, M, N three times larger, you get von Mises back – a good check of the input. In DEFORM you can type the six constants directly – this dialog – or let DEFORM compute them from R-values. The relations are at the bottom: three R-values measured at 0, 45 and 90 degrees define the ratios.
