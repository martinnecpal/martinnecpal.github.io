Most of you will start with the library. Load from library gives you a list by material class, then by grade. The important point: the material is copied into your project. If you edit it, you edit your copy, not the library. When you have measured your own data, use Save in library so the next project starts from your data, not from the generic ones. Export and Import move a material as a file – between colleagues, between the university and a company, or between DEFORM versions. And the warning box: library grades are measured over a certain range of temperature and strain rate, and that range is usually written in the name. If you take a cold-forming curve into a hot forging simulation at 1100 degrees, DEFORM will not complain – it will extrapolate, and the result will be wrong. We come back to extrapolation in block 4.
