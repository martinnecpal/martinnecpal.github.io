For heat treatment, each phase change needs kinetics. Diffusional transformations – ferrite, pearlite, bainite – follow Avrami equations whose constants come from the TTT diagram, extended to continuous cooling by the additivity rule. Martensite is athermal and follows Koistinen–Marburger: the fraction depends only on how far below the Ms temperature you are. Hardness is then a mixture of phase hardnesses, each depending on composition and cooling rate – this is the Hardness tab. TTT data come from dilatometer tests or are computed from composition, for example in SFTC's Material Suite or JMatPro. The pictures: residual hoop stress after quenching a disc – the result of transformation volume change and thermal contraction – and a cold-headed nut, where the hardness distribution comes from strain hardening alone.
