To finish: the limits. The power law is empirical, so it cannot be extrapolated safely in strain, strain rate or temperature. Hardening is unbounded, and pure Hollomon starts at zero stress – in FEM always use the y term or Swift's pre-strain. Rate and temperature enter only through m and temperature tables, so it is not a model for hot forming or impact. And it says nothing about the Bauschinger effect or anisotropy – those come from the yield function and the hardening rule, which we discuss in block 5. The summary in four points is on the left.
