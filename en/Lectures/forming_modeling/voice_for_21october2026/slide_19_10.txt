To close the deep dive, five points. The model is derived from thermally activated dislocation motion. Its exponential term couples temperature and strain rate. The lattice decides the form: BCC – the yield stress is sensitive; FCC – the hardening is sensitive; HCP – both. It is the right choice for high strain rates below about half the melting point: impact, machining, high-speed forming. For hot forming there is the modified, semi-empirical version, and DEFORM has the BCC form built in. Four questions to check yourselves – I will reveal them one by one.
