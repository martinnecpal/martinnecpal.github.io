This is the window. On the left the material list: every material used in the project, and every object – the billet, a die – refers to one of them by number. Top right the switch between Regular and Mixture; under it the Phase buttons that become active for mixtures. Across the middle you see the tabs. Plastic holds the flow stress, yield function and hardening. Elastic – Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio and thermal expansion. Thermal – conductivity, heat capacity, emissivity and density. Then Diffusion, Dislocation, Hardness, Electro-magnetic, Advanced and Texture, which we will touch in block 8. Two practical details. Each value can be a constant or a function of temperature; the pencil icon opens the table. And at the bottom right there is the Unit conversion button – remember it, we need it in three minutes.
