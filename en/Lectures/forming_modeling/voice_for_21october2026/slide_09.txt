Hooke's law, Poisson's ratio and the shear modulus – you know these from strength of materials. In DEFORM you enter E and nu; the shear and bulk moduli follow. Look at the bar chart on a logarithmic scale. Forming metals sit in a narrow band between about 45 and 210 gigapascals; carbide die inserts are three times stiffer than steel, which is exactly why they are used where the die must not deflect. One consequence you will see in the sheet metal lecture: springback scales with stress divided by modulus. Aluminium has a third of the modulus of steel, so for the same stress it springs back about three times more. That is why aluminium car panels are a springback problem.
