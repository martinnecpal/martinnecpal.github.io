Good morning. Last week we compared the forming packages and spent half an hour on DEFORM. Today we open one window in the DEFORM pre-processor – the Material window – and stay there for two hours. That sounds narrow, but almost every number the simulation gives back – press force, temperature, die fill, grain size, cracks – is computed from what you type into that window. By the end of today you should be able to look at a material in DEFORM and say which data it needs for your process, where those data come from, and when you cannot trust them.
