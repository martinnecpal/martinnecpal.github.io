To close the flow-stress block, a decision guide. Start with the process. Cold forming – power law, because hardening dominates. Warm and hot forging – a measured table, or a sinh-Arrhenius model if you have to extrapolate in temperature. Machining – Johnson–Cook or Zerilli–Armstrong. Long holding at temperature – a creep model, which is our block 6. Sheet forming with springback – anisotropic yield and kinematic hardening, which is block 5. And under everything the red line: whatever model you pick, compare the range of strain, strain rate and temperature it was fitted to with the range your process reaches. That is half an hour of material in one sentence.
