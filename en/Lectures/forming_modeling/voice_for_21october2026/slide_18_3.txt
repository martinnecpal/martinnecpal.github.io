Here are constants from the original nineteen eighty-three paper, plus the AISI ten forty-five set we used on the main slide. Note the ranges: A from 90 megapascals for annealed copper to almost 800 for hardened forty-three forty steel; C between 0.006 and 0.06 – so the rate effect is moderate; and m around one, meaning roughly linear softening with temperature. Two warnings. Each set belongs to one material condition – a heat treatment, a hardness – and to the range of the tests it was fitted to. And published sets for the same grade scatter a lot; for AISI ten forty-five alone you will find several quite different sets in the literature. So before you use any set, plot the flow curves at your strain rate and temperature and compare them with whatever data you have.
