Block 8 – data that go beyond mechanics. First, microstructure. During and after hot deformation the material recrystallises, and the recrystallised fraction follows the JMAK or Avrami equation – the S-shaped curves. The time to 50 percent recrystallisation depends on the initial grain size, strain, strain rate and temperature through an Arrhenius term: at 1100 degrees it is a fraction of a second, at 900 degrees tens of seconds. Similar equations give the grain size after dynamic recrystallisation and grain growth during holding. In DEFORM these constants are entered under the Grain button in the Phase area. The result is the picture on the right: grain size in a forged turbine disc. For aerospace discs the customer specifies grain size, not only shape – so this is a design quantity.
