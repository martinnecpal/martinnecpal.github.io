Here is the summary of the whole lecture in one table – keep it for the seminars. Find your process in the left column and read across which data you need. The bold entries are the ones students most often forget: the thermal set for hot forging, the anisotropy and kinematic hardening for springback, and the transformation data for heat treatment. And the four checks at the bottom, for every simulation: units, validity range, temperature dependence, and one validation against a measurement – the press force or a thermocouple. If all four pass, you can start to trust the colourful pictures.
