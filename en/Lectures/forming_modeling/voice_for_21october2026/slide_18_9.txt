Some practical tips for DEFORM. Choose the Johnson–Cook entry in the flow-stress list; with D and E equal to one and alpha equal to zero, DEFORM's extended form reduces to the original. Enter the reference and melting temperatures in the unit of your problem and the reference strain rate of the published set. Run coupled with heat transfer, and plot the curves before running. Summary: three independent brackets – hardening, rate, softening; five constants from quasi-static, Hopkinson-bar and hot tests; strong when combined with adiabatic heating – shear bands, chips, impact; weak where temperature and strain rate interact – hot forming and very high rates. Five questions to check yourselves, then back up to the main lecture or straight on to Zerilli–Armstrong – which, as you will see, was created precisely to fix the missing coupling.
