SLIDE 4 — Axes & Working Space: 5-Axis Configuration
=====================================================

The LASERTEC 80 Shape is a true five-axis system. Let me walk through the axis layout.

The three linear axes give a working volume of 800 mm in X, 500 mm in Y, and 700 mm in Z. The Z axis is also the focusing axis — moving the laser head closer or farther from the workpiece changes the focal position.

Beyond the three linear axes, the machine has two rotational axes. The B axis is a swivel axis with a range from minus 110 to plus 150 degrees. The C axis provides full 360-degree rotation. Together, B and C allow the laser beam to reach virtually any surface orientation on a complex three-dimensional workpiece — this is what enables five-axis laser structuring.

The machine offers two table configurations. In three-axis mode, the table measures 900 by 600 millimetres and can carry up to 200 kilograms. In five-axis mode, a rotary-tilt table is used — available in two sizes: 200 mm diameter for loads up to 14 kg, or 400 mm diameter for loads up to 40 kg.

The scanner head above the table can deflect the laser beam within a range of plus or minus 60 mm without moving the machine axes. Workpieces are held using EROWA clamping systems, which you can see in the interior photo on the right.
