SLIDE 2 — Overview & Context: What is the LASERTEC 80 Shape?
==============================================================

The LASERTEC 80 Shape is a five-axis precision CNC laser machining system produced by DMG MORI. It is installed in our Centre of Excellence for 5-Axis Machining — CE5AM — at MTF STU Trnava.

The machine was purpose-built for four main tasks. First, laser structuring and engraving of injection molds — creating fine surface patterns directly on mold cavities. Second, laser micro-machining and material ablation — removing very thin layers of material with high precision. Third, functional surface texturing for tribological or decorative purposes — for example, to improve friction behaviour between sliding components. And fourth, manufacturing chip-breakers on cutting inserts made from sintered carbide.

It is worth noting that our faculty operates two laser systems. Besides the LASERTEC 80 Shape, we also have a TruDisk 4002 from TRUMPF, which uses a different laser principle. The LASERTEC 80 Shape is the five-axis machine suited for complex three-dimensional surfaces.

On the photo you can see the machine with its characteristic blue enclosure, the Siemens 840D CNC control panel mounted in front, and the standalone laser control PC to the right.
